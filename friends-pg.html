PG rules

A few friendly house rules so everyone can enjoy their stay. They match the notice board at the PG.

  1. Rent is due between the 5th and 10th of every month. A quick reminder so we all stay on track!
  2. Planning to move out? Just let us know before the 25th. If we don’t hear from you by then, next month’s rent will apply too.
  3. Deposits are non-refundable, so please keep that in mind when you join.
  4. When you join, please hand in a photocopy of your ID proof. It helps us keep everyone safe.
  5. Room charges are paid by the person staying in the room. Please don’t route them through your company. Thanks for understanding!
  6. Treat your room like your own home. You’re responsible for it from move-in to move-out, including any damage.
  7. Let’s keep our PG calm, clean and tidy. Thanks for helping make it a great place for everyone!
